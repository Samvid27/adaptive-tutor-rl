The Setup

Imagine a student learning 5 topics. Each question, you choose which topic and how hard it is. Too easy and they don't learn much means they already knew it. Too hard and they also don't learn much means pure frustration. The sweet spot is a question just slightly above what they currently know.

This runs a 30-question simulated session using six strategies against the same simulated student, measured by real underlying skill.

Strategies Compared

DQN_AGENT
Learns from a reused replay buffer of past experience. The strongest performer here.
PPO_AGENT
Learns from fresh batches, updated cautiously each round.
A2C_AGENT
Simpler on-policy algorithm, less conservative than PPO.
ORACLE
"Cheats" by seeing true hidden skill directly. The best any strategy could do.
HEURISTIC
Weakest-topic, fixed difficulty. Doesn't adapt difficulty.
RANDOM
No strategy at all. The worst case.
Each number generates a different simulated student.
